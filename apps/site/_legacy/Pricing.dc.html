Billing questions

What counts as a machine?

One running daemon. A laptop, a workstation, an EC2 box — each is one machine, regardless of how many tmux sessions it hosts.

Does the free tier expire?

No. Solo is free indefinitely and includes agent notifications. We'd rather you upgrade because you bought a second machine.

Can I pay and still self-host everything?

Yes — Pro and Team include the relay image. Licence checks are offline-tolerant for 30 days so an air-gapped install keeps working.

Refunds?

Ask within 30 days and we refund, no questions. Cancel any time; you keep Pro until the period ends, then fall back to Solo.
