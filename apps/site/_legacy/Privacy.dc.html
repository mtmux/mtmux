Privacy policy

Last updated 18 July 2026. Plain language; no dark patterns.

The short version

Your terminal contents are encrypted end-to-end between your machine and your devices. We cannot read them, and we do not store them. Everything below is detail on the small amount of metadata that makes the service work.

What we collect

Account data — only if you pay: email, billing country, and a Stripe customer id. Card details never touch our servers.
Relay metadata — connection timestamps, device fingerprint hashes, byte counts and error codes, kept 30 days for abuse prevention and debugging.
Notification delivery — event level, session name and timestamp, kept for the retention window on your plan (7 days, 90 days or 1 year). Bodies are stored only if you enable history.
Website analytics — aggregate page counts with no cookies, no cross-site identifiers and no ad networks.

What we never collect

Terminal output, keystrokes, file contents, paths or environment variables.
Your source code, in any form, at any time.
SSH keys — mtmux never asks for one.

Third parties

Stripe (payments), Apple and Google push services (notification delivery), and our hosting provider for the relay. Anything you route yourself — Slack, Discord, a webhook — is governed by that service's terms, and receives exactly the content you configure.

Push bodies pass through Apple or Google. Set include_tail = 0 if you don't want pane output in them.

Your rights

Email privacy@mtmux.com to export or delete everything associated with your account; we complete requests within 30 days. Self-hosted installs hold their own data, and we never see it.

Changes

Material changes are announced in the changelog and by email to account holders at least 14 days before taking effect.
