Changelog — mtmux releases
// changelog

What shipped.

Roughly every two weeks. Breaking changes get a major bump and a migration note.

0.9.4 18 July 2026 · current
newReply from notification: blocked events carry key actions, so y/n answers never need the session.
newPresets for Gemini CLI and aider; --explain shows why a watcher fired.
perfCell-diff encoder rewritten: 38% fewer bytes on full-screen redraws.
fixiOS 18 keyboard no longer steals the first keypress after reattach.
0.9.0 27 June 2026
newAgent watchers: mtmux watch --agents with done / blocked / stalled / failed levels.
newRouting to Slack, Discord, ntfy, gotify and signed webhooks; quiet hours and dedupe.
breakmtmux serve is now mtmux up. The old name warns until 0.11.
0.8.2 6 June 2026
newTablet split mode with drag-resizable gutters and full Bluetooth chord passthrough.
newSixel and iTerm2 inline image rendering.
fixCopy-mode selections now reach the device clipboard on Android Firefox.
0.8.0 15 May 2026
newSelf-hosted relay images on ghcr, with a fingerprint allow-list.
newRead-only share links with TTL.
perfSession key rotation moved off the render path; reattach is ~120ms faster.
0.7.0 3 April 2026 · first public release
newAttach to a running tmux server from any browser, with pane reflow and the modifier row.
newDevice pairing with one-time codes, per-device revocation, LAN mode.
Subscribe to releases on GitHub, or run mtmux upgrade — it tells you what changed before it replaces the binary.
